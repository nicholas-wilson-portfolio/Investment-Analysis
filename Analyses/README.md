# Investment-Analysis
This repository contains structured investment analyses using fundamental and technical metrics to evaluate growth potential and market mispricing.
## Overview
Each analysis determines a grade /100 based on a consistent framework that looks at:\
• Business performance and expected growth\
• Financial strength\
• Price trends and market expectations

The goal is not to "pick winners", but to practice making structured, reasoned investment decisions. The scores and conclusions reflect my own learning and interpretation, **not investment advice.**
## Fundamental Metrics Used
• Earnings trend TTM\
• Institutional ownership\
• P/B vs industry\
• Revenue growth trend YoY\
• Margin Expansion\
• Free cash flow\
• Short interest
## Technical Indicators Used
• Support and resistance zones\
• Chart patterns\
• Price vs MAs\
• RSI (14)\
• Volume
## Position Sizing and Risk
Based on the grade received, companies are grouped into three categories: strong buy, moderate buy, and watchlist/avoid. Position sizing is based on risk level, volatility, and confidence in the analysis.

This helps practice risk management and build consistent investing patterns.
